## Design Context

### Users
Thai readers who want a fast, credible buying guide they can open on a phone and share with friends before buying a BGG umbrella online. Their job is to compare sun/UV protection, rain readiness, wind resistance, portability, and value without reading several product pages.

### Brand Personality
Practical, evidence-led, and calm. The page should feel like a precise buyer's guide from a trusted friend, not like an ad or a marketplace listing.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial comparison dashboard with a bright outdoor palette, product photography, dense but readable tables, and clear recommendation chips. The design should avoid generic hero marketing, oversized decorative cards, and vague claims.

### Design Principles
- Lead with the buying decision, then show the evidence.
- Make mobile comparison comfortable: short labels, sticky cues, and scannable score bars.
- Separate confirmed specs from judgment calls and caveats.
- Use color sparingly for meaning: sun, rain, wind, portability, and value each get a stable cue.
- Keep every section shareable as a standalone answer.
